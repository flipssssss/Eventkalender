Monday, 03.12 from 7pm -  End of Year(ning)
END OF THE YEAR(NING)💥
A drag show hosted by your favourite local nightmare TRANSMASCULA, and visiting from the northern darkness, your sexy chaos RAXA. Featuring a diverse lineup of
 artists ready to deliver:
* BROSKI • DANNY CRISIS • FEMME BOYANT • NETTY BABY • RUSTY PIPES • 
The darkest season is upon us, and the queers of Berlin are all in their peak yearly yearning, desperately craving for skin, sin, and some winter lovin’. In the
 midst of the dark murky December and an even darker political landscape, we are all looking for a promise of lighter days to come. We have the medicine you are looking for, and it is not drugs,
 Christmas carols or endless consumption, but the one and only power of drag.
The year is coming to an end, and with this drag show, so is the yearning. Whether you’re longing to escape the darkness of the Berlin winter or embrace it in all
 its devastating gloom, we swear: YOU ARE GOING TO GET SOME. Expect creative weirdos, political statements and some classic satire, this show will make you wetter than the slushy streets of
 Neukölln. 
📍 Location: Silver Future Bar (Weserstr. 206)
🗓️ Date: December 3rd
🚪 Doors open: 17:00
🎭 Show starts: 19:00
🎟️ Free of charge!
Please make sure to bring CASH — it’s the only way to support the artists directly. Tip generously and keep queer art alive 💸💋
Come early. Stay loud. Support drag.
